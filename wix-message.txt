Title:
Cardiac MRI Hub has moved

Post:
All the planning guides from this site now live in the Cardiac MRI Hub app:

https://app.cardiacmrihub.workers.dev/

It's free, there's nothing to sign up for, and it works on your phone, tablet or the
computer at the console. What's new:

- Every guide, with its images and cine clips, in one place
- Quick plan: swipe through the planning images for any view while you're at the scanner
- LV parameters at a glance: the settings for every LV view on one page
- Search across all the guides
- Works offline: add it to your home screen and save the guides for scan rooms with no signal

How to put it on your phone (takes a minute): https://app.cardiacmrihub.workers.dev/guide/install-the-app/

This site will stay up for now, but new guides and updates will only appear in the app,
so please update your bookmarks.

Spotted something wrong or out of date? Every guide has a "Suggest a correction" link.

For education only. Always follow your department's protocols and your radiologist or cardiologist.
