Cardiac MRI Hub installs straight from your browser: no app store, no account, nothing to pay. Once it's installed it has its own icon, opens full-screen like any other app, and keeps working where there's no signal.

# iPhone and iPad

1. Open **app.cardiacmrihub.workers.dev** in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**. On iOS 26, tap the **•••** button next to the address bar first, then **Share**.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. If you see **Open as Web App**, leave it switched on.
5. Tap **Add**.

# Android

1. Open **app.cardiacmrihub.workers.dev** in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app**. If you only see **Add to Home screen**, tap that, and choose **Install** if it asks.

When your phone allows it, the app also shows an **Install** button on its home screen that does the same thing.

# Computer

- **Chrome or Edge:** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or look for **Install** in the browser's menu.
- **Safari on a Mac:** choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.

# Use it at the scanner without signal

Every guide you open is saved on your device automatically. To have every guide, image and cine clip ready before you need them, tap the gear at the top of the app's home screen to open **Settings**, then tap **Save everything for offline**. It's about 30 MB, so Wi-Fi is best.

# Updates

There's nothing to update by hand. When you're online, the app checks for new and updated guides each time you open it, and app updates install themselves the next time you open it.

To remove the app, press and hold its icon and remove it like any other app.
