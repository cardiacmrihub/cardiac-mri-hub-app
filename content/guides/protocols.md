Each protocol lists the sequences in the order you run them, with links to how each view is planned. They follow the Society for Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance's [standardized protocols (2020 update)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7038611/). Your department's protocol may differ, and it's the one to follow. Stress agents and contrast doses aren't listed here: they follow your department's protocol and the supervising physician.

Every exam starts with the same localizers, listed first. The order here runs the cines before contrast; some sites give contrast earlier and run the cines while they wait for LGE. At the scanner, open the [exam flow](/flow/) to tick the steps off as you go, with a clock that starts when you inject contrast.

# Every exam starts with

1. 3-plane localizer
2. Axial bright-blood stack of the chest
3. Axial dark-blood stack of the chest
4. Heart scouts

# Cardiomyopathy

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex
3. Cine 4-chamber stack
4. T1 map: [short-axis samples](/guide/lv-short-axis-samples/) (base, mid, apex). See [MOLLI maps](/guide/molli-maps/).
5. T2 map: short-axis samples

**Contrast**

1. Perfusion: short-axis samples, during the contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. See [choosing the TI time](/guide/psir-ti-time/).
2. LGE PSIR: 2-, 3- and 4-chamber
3. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack
4. LGE PSIR: 4-chamber stack
5. T1 map: short-axis samples, 15 minutes after contrast, for ECV (optional)

# Myocarditis

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex
3. T2 map or T2-weighted (STIR): [short-axis samples](/guide/lv-short-axis-samples/), for edema
4. T1 map: short-axis samples

**Contrast**

1. Contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. Early gadolinium enhancement, 1–3 minutes after contrast (optional)
2. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. See [choosing the TI time](/guide/psir-ti-time/).
3. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack, then 2-, 3- and 4-chamber
4. T1 map: short-axis samples, 15 minutes after contrast, for ECV (optional)

# Acute myocardial infarction

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex
3. T2 map or T2-weighted: [short-axis samples](/guide/lv-short-axis-samples/), for edema (optional)

**Contrast**

1. Perfusion: short-axis samples, during the contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. Early gadolinium enhancement, 1–3 minutes after contrast, for microvascular obstruction (optional)
2. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. See [choosing the TI time](/guide/psir-ti-time/).
3. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack, then 2-, 3- and 4-chamber
4. Long-TI LGE if thrombus is a question: about 550 ms at 1.5 T, 850 ms at 3 T (optional)

# Viability

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex
3. Low-dose dobutamine cines for contractile reserve, with the supervising physician (optional)

**Contrast**

1. Contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. See [choosing the TI time](/guide/psir-ti-time/).
2. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack, then 2-, 3- and 4-chamber

# Stress perfusion

**Before stress**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/). Can also be run in the wait between stress and rest.
2. Plan the perfusion slices: at least 3 [short-axis samples](/guide/lv-short-axis-samples/) (base, mid, apex)

**Stress**

1. Stress agent, given by your department's protocol with the supervising physician
2. Stress perfusion: short-axis samples, during the contrast injection at peak stress

**Between stress and rest**

1. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex, and any long axes not done yet
2. Rest perfusion: same slices, at least 10 minutes after the stress injection (optional)

**After rest**

1. TI scout, at least 5 minutes after the rest injection, about 15 minutes after the stress injection
2. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack, then 2-, 3- and 4-chamber

# Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex
3. If the LVOT is obstructed: flow across the LVOT in the 3-chamber view, for turbulence, systolic anterior motion and the gradient. See [phase contrast planning](/guide/phase-contrast-planning/).
4. T1 map and T2 map: [short-axis samples](/guide/lv-short-axis-samples/) (optional)

**Contrast**

1. Contrast injection, with stress perfusion only if ischemia is a question

**After contrast**

1. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. See [choosing the TI time](/guide/psir-ti-time/).
2. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack, then 2-, 3- and 4-chamber
3. T1 map: short-axis samples, 15 minutes after contrast, for ECV (optional)

# Arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy (ARVC)

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex, with 5–6 mm slices
3. Cine [axial stack through the RV](/guide/rv-axial/) and RVOT, 5–6 mm
4. Cine [RV inflow](/guide/rv-inflow/) and [RVOT](/guide/rvot/)
5. Black-blood T1 FSE, then again with fat suppression (optional)

**Contrast**

1. Contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. Check the TI nulls the RV wall too.
2. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack, 2-, 3- and 4-chamber, and the axial RV views

# Sarcoidosis

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex
3. T2 map or T2-weighted: [short-axis samples](/guide/lv-short-axis-samples/)
4. T1 map: short-axis samples

**Contrast**

1. Contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. See [choosing the TI time](/guide/psir-ti-time/).
2. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack, then 2-, 3- and 4-chamber

# Iron overload

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex
3. T2*: one mid [short-axis](/guide/lv-short-axis-samples/) slice, before any contrast
4. Axial T2* through the liver (optional)

**Contrast (only if the LV or RV ejection fraction is abnormal)**

1. Contrast injection (optional)

**After contrast**

1. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast (optional)
2. LGE PSIR: short-axis stack, then 2-, 3- and 4-chamber (optional)

# Pericardial disease

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex
3. Black-blood T1 or T2 FSE: 2–3 long-axis and 3–4 short-axis slices, to measure pericardial thickness (normal is 3 mm or less)
4. Tagged cine, if the pericardium is thickened (optional)
5. Real-time cine, mid short axis, through at least two deep breaths, for septal bounce

**Contrast**

1. Contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. See [choosing the TI time](/guide/psir-ti-time/).
2. LGE, with and without fat saturation

# Cardiac mass or thrombus

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/), plus cines through the mass
2. T1 FSE through the mass
3. T2 FSE with fat suppression through the mass

**Contrast**

1. Perfusion through the mass, during the contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. T1 FSE with fat suppression, early after contrast
2. Cines through the mass again (optional)
3. TI scout, 10 minutes after contrast. See [choosing the TI time](/guide/psir-ti-time/).
4. LGE PSIR through the mass and the heart
5. Long-TI LGE to null thrombus: about 500–550 ms at 1.5 T, 850–900 ms at 3 T

# Valves

**No contrast needed**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/)
2. Cine [short-axis stack](/guide/lv-short-axis/), base to apex, for volumes
3. Cines through the valve in question: [aortic](/guide/aortic-valve/), [mitral](/guide/mitral-valve/), [tricuspid](/guide/tricuspid-valve/) or [pulmonary](/guide/pulmonary-valve/)
4. Flow just above the valve (about 5 mm), through-plane. For stenosis, place it at the peak of the jet. See [phase contrast planning](/guide/phase-contrast-planning/).
5. Mitral regurgitation: flow at the sinotubular junction, compared with LV stroke volume
6. Aortic regurgitation: flow in the descending aorta too, for diastolic flow reversal (optional)

Set the VENC as low as it will go without aliasing, and raise it if the phase images alias.

# Thoracic aorta

**Before contrast**

1. Sagittal oblique cine along the aorta ([candy cane view](/guide/thoracic-aorta/))
2. Flow at the [aortic valve](/guide/aortic-valve/), if the valve is part of the question (optional)
3. Black-blood T1 FSE or spoiled GRE through the aorta, if looking for hematoma or dissection (optional)

**Contrast**

1. Contrast MRA during the injection, timed with a test bolus or bolus tracking

**After contrast**

1. A second MRA pass

# Pulmonary veins before ablation

**Before contrast**

1. Cine [2-chamber](/guide/lv-2-chamber/), [3-chamber](/guide/lv-3-chamber/) and [4-chamber](/guide/lv-4-chamber/) (optional)
2. Coronal 3D MRA covering the pulmonary veins and left atrium, before contrast

**Contrast**

1. First-pass 3D MRA during the contrast injection

**After contrast**

1. Delayed 3D MRA pass (optional)
2. Flow in each pulmonary vein, or LGE of the left atrial wall (optional)
