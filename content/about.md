Cardiac MRI Hub is a free set of step-by-step cardiac MRI planning guides for technologists, made to be used at the scanner: on your phone, and offline when the control room has no signal.

# Who writes it

The guides are written by a cardiac MRI technologist with years of hands-on experience in outpatient imaging, level I trauma centers and advanced cardiovascular imaging centers. That work has included building cardiac programs, refining protocols and workflows, contributing to research studies, and training many technologists who were new to cardiac.

# Why it exists

A cardiac exam is only as good as its planning. When each view is planned precisely, the images line up, the measurements hold, and the reading physician gets what they need. Cardiac MRI Hub shares practical, repeatable ways to plan each view, the settings that matter, and the reasoning behind them.

It's for technologists learning cardiac MRI or sharpening their skills, and for imaging programs starting or growing a cardiac service.

# Sources

The guides come from hands-on scanning and teaching, checked against these references. Each one is worth your time:

- **[SCMR standardized CMR protocols, 2020 update](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7038611/).** The Society for Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance's recommended protocols by indication, which the [Protocols](/guide/protocols/) page follows. Kramer CM, Barkhausen J, Bucciarelli-Ducci C, Flamm SD, Kim RJ, Nagel E. Journal of Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance 2020;22:17.
- **[Expert consensus on imaging cardiac amyloidosis, part 1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12350-019-01760-6).** The multisociety recommendations, endorsed by SCMR, that the amyloidosis protocol follows, since the SCMR protocols don't cover it. Dorbala S et al. Journal of Nuclear Cardiology 2019;26:2065–2123.
- **[Duke Cardiovascular MR Center courses](https://dcmrc.thinkific.com).** Free online courses, including CMR for Technologists, Introduction to CMR and 25 CMR Cases.
- **[CardiacMRI.com](https://cardiacmri.com).** A free learning center with technical and disease guides, sample reports, case reviews, self-tests and video lectures.
- **[Visible Heart Laboratories, University of Minnesota](https://med.umn.edu/vhlab).** The Atlas of Human Cardiac Anatomy and other free tools for seeing how the heart is built.

Cardiac MRI Hub isn't affiliated with any of these organizations.
